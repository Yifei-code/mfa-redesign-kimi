Talent Programs

Excellence Shouldn't Have to Choose

Athletics · Music & Arts — full academics on a performer's schedule

01 — Student-Athlete Program

Built for the Training Calendar

Meridian Future Academy helps student-athletes balance rigorous academics with demanding training, travel and competition schedules through flexible, self-paced coursework — without compromising the diploma's academic weight.

  • Self-paced courses that flex around training and travel
  • Full 21-credit, Cognia-accredited diploma track
  • Official transcripts and records for college recruiting
  • College counseling attuned to athletic timelines

02 — Music & Arts Program

Where Musicians Keep Practicing

Serious young musicians and artists need hours academics rarely respect. MFA's flexible schedule protects rehearsal, studio and performance time while master classes deepen the craft.

  • Master classes in Music — performance, theory and audition preparation
  • Master classes in Art & Drama — portfolio-ready creative development
  • Chess master classes — strategic thinking as a competitive discipline
  • Graduates admitted to institutions such as Berklee College of Music

Practice Hours Are Sacred

Coursework on Canvas LMS travels with the student — conservatory audition season, tournament travel, or a recording session never costs a credit.

Format Self-paced Credit Full U.S. diploma
