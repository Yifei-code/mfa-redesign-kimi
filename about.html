About the Academy

Education for a Future Worth Inheriting

Massachusetts-registered · Cognia accredited · Boston—Singapore

Mission

We are committed to cultivating self-disciplined thinkers, decision-makers with moral judgment, and global citizens capable of addressing future challenges.

Vision

Under a credible international accreditation framework, integrating institutional governance, academic rigor, and innovative digital teaching to redefine secondary education in the global era.

Institutional Foundation

Fifteen Years of Academic Leadership

Meridian Future Academy, formerly known as Wayland Academy, is an international school registered in Massachusetts, USA, dedicated to integrating cutting-edge technology into the K-12 stage to create a future-oriented learning experience.

The school is built upon over fifteen years of experience in international secondary education project management and academic leadership.

  • Registered entity in Massachusetts, USA
  • 15+ years of international secondary education experience
  • Global accreditation & international perspective

Our Commitment

Education should transcend the classroom — cultivating global leaders who can shape the future and serve society. Meridian Future Academy

Through a rigorous academic system, personalized college counseling, and a deep moral foundation, we open the doors to the world's top universities for every student — leading to a globally recognized U.S. high-school diploma.
