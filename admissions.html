02 — Application Form

Enrollment Application

Apply Online in About 15 Minutes

Our application is hosted on a secure online form platform. It walks you through six sections — basic information, contact details, academic background, language & scores, family information, and a personal statement — with bilingual (English / 中文) guidance throughout.

Start the Application

Applications are reviewed in the order received; our admissions office will contact you within 3–5 business days. Questions: admissions@meridianfuture.org
