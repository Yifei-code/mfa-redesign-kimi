01 — Who We Are

An Accredited American School, Without Borders

Meridian Future Academy is an independently operated, internationally accredited high school serving Grades 9–12. We offer a systematic college-preparatory program for students with global higher-education goals.

Two Hemispheres, One School

Academic headquarters in Boston within the Boston education corridor; operational headquarters in Singapore — supporting pathways to universities in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, Hong Kong, China, and beyond.

05 — Talent Programs

Built Around Exceptional Students

Dedicated tracks for student-athletes and young musicians — rigorous academics on a schedule that respects training, travel, rehearsal and performance.

Student-Athlete Program

Flexible, self-paced coursework that balances demanding training and competition calendars with a full U.S. college-preparatory education. 82% of MFA student-athletes are admitted to their first-choice college.

Music & Arts Program

Master classes in music, art, drama and chess, paired with portfolio-ready academic records — MFA graduates have gone on to institutions such as Berklee College of Music.
