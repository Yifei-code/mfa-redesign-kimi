Accreditation & Compliance

A Credential You Can Verify

Cognia accredited · Independent diploma-granting authority

01 — Cognia Accreditation

Globally Recognized

Meridian Future Academy is accredited by Cognia, meeting international standards for secondary-education governance, teaching and continuous improvement.

Cognia is the world's largest education accreditation agency. Accreditation means the school's educational quality, governance structure and student-support systems have reached international standards.

02 — School Credentials

The Institution Behind the Diploma

Independent Diploma-Granting Authority

Authorized to issue international high-school diplomas, globally recognized.

Board of Trustees Governance

Ensuring institutional integrity, academic quality and accreditation compliance.

Permanent Academic Records

All student records securely stored at the Massachusetts headquarters.

Official Transcript Issuance

Standardized transcript management supporting global university applications.

03 — Standards

Cognia Accreditation Standards

  • Leadership and governance
  • Learning environment and culture
  • Student learning and growth
  • Resource management and sustainability
  • Continuous improvement mechanisms

Compliance

Compliance Assurance

  • Annual audit reports
  • Board oversight
  • Academic integrity policies
  • Student record security
  • Transcript verification mechanisms

Accreditation Compliance Commitment: MFA strictly adheres to Cognia standards and undergoes regular evaluation; governance, academic programs and student support all operate within the accreditation framework.
