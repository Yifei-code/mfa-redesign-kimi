About MFA

What kind of school is MFA?
MFA is a Cognia-accredited U.S. high-school curriculum system and education-technology provider. We offer U.S. high-school courses, an online classroom (Canvas LMS), and AI teaching tools (AI English Classroom, AI Tools for Teachers) to students, partner schools, international education institutions, and college counseling services.
Is MFA internationally accredited?
Yes. The MFA curriculum is accredited by Cognia (formerly AdvancED), meeting U.S. high-school educational standards. Our graduation diploma is widely recognized by universities worldwide.

Courses & Credits

Which grades and subjects are covered?
We cover Grades 9–12, offering foundational courses, innovative courses (AI, Scientific Literacy, PBL), advanced honors-level courses (mathematics, sciences, computer science, statistics, English literature and more), and master classes (art, music, drama, chess).
How many credits are required for graduation?
A minimum of 21 credits is required, including core requirements, innovative requirements (Scientific Literacy, AI Literacy, PBL-Capstone), and electives. Please refer to the Academics page for details.
Do you offer advanced courses?
Yes. We offer honors-level and advanced courses across mathematics, sciences, computer science, statistics and English literature, matched to each student's academic track — all transcripted under the same Cognia-accredited standards.

Online Classroom & Canvas

How do students take classes?
Students participate in online learning and submit assignments via mfa.canvaslms.net (Canvas LMS). AI learning tools are accessed through the student's unified MFA account.
Is there a Single Sign-On (SSO) system?
MFA uses meridianfuture.org domain email as the unified identity for logging into all MFA products, including AI tools and Canvas.

Graduation & College

What diploma do students receive upon graduation?
Upon completing the credit requirements, students receive an MFA U.S. High School Diploma (Cognia accredited), which can be used when applying to universities worldwide.
Is college counseling provided?
Yes. We provide college counseling based on course grades and project portfolios to help students plan and apply to universities overseas.

Didn't Find Your Answer?

Our admissions and partnership team is happy to answer any questions about courses, credits, partnerships and college admissions.
