02 — Academic Standards

Standards That Travel

4.0 GPA System

The standard U.S. 4.0 grade-point system, fully consistent with U.S. domestic high schools — A (4.0) to F (0) — providing an authoritative academic evaluation basis for applications to top universities in the U.S. and globally.

  • A (90–100): 4.0 · Excellent
  • B (80–89): 3.0 · Good
  • C (70–79): 2.0 · Average
  • D (60–69): 1.0 · Passing
  • F (below 60): 0 · Failing

Standardized Grading

All courses use unified grading standards to ensure authenticity and comparability. Teachers evaluate against clear rubrics spanning multiple dimensions.

  • Class participation and performance
  • Homework and project completion
  • Quizzes and unit tests
  • Midterm and final exams
  • Learning-progress margin

Official Transcript Management

A complete academic record for every student, managed uniformly by the Massachusetts administrative headquarters. Standard U.S.-format transcripts can be used directly for college applications.

  • Sealed and issued by the school
  • Supports WES, ECE and other credential evaluations
  • Permanent records stored at U.S. headquarters

Academic Integrity Policy

Strict academic integrity standards cultivate students' academic moral awareness. All students sign an integrity pledge; cheating and plagiarism are severely sanctioned.

  • Signed academic integrity pledge
  • Turnitin plagiarism detection
  • Fully proctored examinations
  • Violations recorded in student file
  • Correct citation and originality training

All academic standards meet Cognia accreditation requirements, ensuring global recognition of MFA transcripts.

03 — Advanced & Extension

Beyond the Core

Students participate in advanced courses and research projects that strengthen college applications and independent thinking.
